Welcome!

I am a PhD student at the University of Arizona's Lunar and Planetary Laboratory. My current research focuses on the early evolution of Mars. Using a variety of modeling techniques, I investigate the geologic activity that shaped the surface of ancient Mars. I am especially fascianted by water driven processes, and I am driven to understand how the Martian climate and water inventory has changed over time. Before graduate school, I worked as a research technologist on the OSIRIS-REx asteroid sample return mission. I developed a laboratory-based imaging system that was used to image the sample of asteroid Bennu after its delivery to earth in 2023. I began this work on OSIRIS-REx while finishing my Bachelor of Science in Astronomy at the University of Arizona. As an undergraduate, I worked on sub-millimeter and radio astronomy research.
